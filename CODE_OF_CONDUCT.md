# Code of Conduct

Aftercare aims to be a respectful, constructive project for people with different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:
- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementation rather than people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's personal information.
- Accept that maintainers may reject changes that do not fit the project's scope, safety requirements or maintenance capacity.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable. The same standards apply in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports should be sent privately through the contact method on the maintainer's public GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as practical. The maintainer may edit or remove unacceptable contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation.

This policy applies to project spaces and when someone is officially representing the project.
